      Moonlight Roller Rink is the original music project of songwriter, vocalist, guitarist, and Washington, DC native Paul B.

      Most songs have a different assortment of musicians on each recording, but several are repeat players and collaborators, including Richmond, Virginia based musician Matt Waller. Quite literally musical chairs with some of the area’s best, Moonlight Roller Rink is a collective composition of friends and fellow music-makers creating art from many unique perspectives, ideas, and personal experiences.
